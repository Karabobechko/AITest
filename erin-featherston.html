Meet Erin

Food that nourishes body and soul

Made with care and intention.

I’m a personal chef based in Barrie, Ontario, serving clients across Simcoe County, Muskoka and the GTA.

A Culinary Management graduate of George Brown College, I’ve spent more than 15 years working in restaurants, bakeries and catering, and teaching hands-on cooking classes.

Today, I specialize in healthy, customized meals for busy professionals and families, using fresh, locally sourced, seasonal ingredients.
